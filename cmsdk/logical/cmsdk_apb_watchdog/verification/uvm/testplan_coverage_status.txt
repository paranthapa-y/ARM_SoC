Implemented tests created so far
--------------------------------
1. wdog_reset_test / wdog_reset_seq
   - Covers reset/default-state checks
   - Aligns to WDOG_RST_01 through WDOG_RST_07

2. wdog_lock_test / wdog_lock_seq
   - Covers lock/unlock and write-protection behavior
   - Aligns to WDOG_LOCK_01, WDOG_LOCK_02, WDOG_LOCK_03, WDOG_LOCK_06

3. wdog_load_control_test / wdog_load_control_seq
   - Covers load programming and control register behavior
   - Aligns to WDOG_LOAD_01, WDOG_LOAD_02, WDOG_LOAD_03, WDOG_LOAD_04 and WDOG_CTRL_01 through WDOG_CTRL_05

4. wdog_interrupt_flow_test / wdog_interrupt_flow_seq
   - Covers first-timeout interrupt flow and clear/reload behavior
   - Aligns to WDOG_FLOW_01 through WDOG_FLOW_04 and WDOG_CLR_01, WDOG_CLR_03

5. wdog_sanity_test / wdog_sanity_seq
   - End-to-end watchdog flow check for a representative full scenario
   - Covers a combined sanity flow based on the documented watchdog behavior
